Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what people usually ask us

Who is eligible to donate blood?

Most healthy adults between 18 and 60 years old, weighing at least 50kg, can donate. A quick health check is usually done at the donation site before you give blood.

How often can I donate blood?

Generally, you can donate whole blood once every 3 to 4 months. This gives your body enough time to replenish blood cells safely.

Is my personal information safe on Blood Bridge?

Yes. Your details are only used to help match you with people in need of blood and are not shared with anyone outside the platform.

What should I do in a blood emergency?

Click the "Emergency" button in the navigation bar to submit an urgent blood request with the blood group needed and your contact details.

Does it cost anything to register or search for donors?

No. Blood Bridge is completely free to use for both donors and people searching for blood.
