Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before using Blood Bridge.

1. About This Platform

Blood Bridge is a student project built to help connect blood donors with people who need blood. It is not a hospital, a certified blood bank, or a medical service provider, and it does not replace professional medical advice or emergency services.

2. Accuracy of Information

When you register as a donor or submit a blood request, you agree to provide accurate and truthful information. Blood Bridge does not verify donor eligibility, blood group accuracy, or medical history, so any arrangement made between a donor and a requester should be confirmed through proper medical channels before donation.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

This project stores account details, donor information, and blood requests locally in your browser using localStorage. Data is not sent to an external server or database, and it does not persist across different browsers or devices.

4. User Responsibilities

Users agree not to misuse the platform, submit false information, or use contact details found on Blood Bridge for any purpose unrelated to blood donation.

5. Limitation of Liability

Blood Bridge is provided as-is, as an academic project. We are not liable for any outcome resulting from a donor-requester connection made through this platform, including missed donations, incorrect information, or medical complications.
