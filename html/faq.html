Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about registering, searching donors, and using Blood Bridge.

Who can register as a donor?

Most healthy adults aged 18 and above who meet the local donation criteria can register and help save lives.

How does the search feature work?

You can search by blood group and location to find potential donors quickly and reach out to them directly.

Is the platform free to use?

Yes. Blood Bridge is designed to help people connect without any charge for basic donor and request services.

What should I do in an emergency?

Use the Emergency link in the navigation bar to submit an urgent request with the required blood group and contact details.
